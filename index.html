Mystery Route Studio

Mystery Route Studio is the private production tool of Mystery Route - เส้นทางปริศนา, a Thai channel of short documentary videos about real mysteries from around the world (YouTube: @MysteryRouteTH).

The channel owner uses it on their own computer to:

  • upload and schedule the channel's own videos on its YouTube channel, with the altered or synthetic content label set;
  • reply to comments on the channel's own videos;
  • read the channel's own YouTube Analytics for its weekly report.

It is not offered to the public and works only with the Mystery Route channel. No other user signs in to it.
