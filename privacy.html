Privacy policy

Mystery Route Studio ("the app") is the private production tool of the Mystery Route - เส้นทางปริศนา channel. Only the channel owner signs in to it, with the owner's own Google account.

What the app accesses

Through Google's OAuth sign-in and the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, the app accesses only the Mystery Route channel: its channel details, its videos (to upload and schedule them and set their details), comments on its videos (to read and reply), and its analytics reports.

How the data is used and kept

  • The data is used only to run the channel: posting its videos, answering its viewers and writing its weekly report.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file only the owner's user account can read. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The owner works with an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) that reads this data on the owner's behalf to draft the channel's replies and reports. Reports are kept on the owner's own storage. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps & services. The owner can also delete the stored token from the owner's computer.

Contact

Questions: the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen, or a message to the Mystery Route Facebook Page.
